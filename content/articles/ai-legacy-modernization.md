A legacy application preserves years of business decisions across code, data, integrations, configuration, and people's workarounds. Replacing it safely starts with understanding that accumulated knowledge.

AI can help uncover candidate rules, dependencies, and contradictions. The findings need business and technology review.

## Validate understanding early

For each consequential requirement, connect the observed legacy behaviour to a reviewed decision and a way to verify the replacement. Use scenarios and prototypes to resolve ambiguity while the design can still change cheaply.

This exposes hidden scope, focuses expert attention, and strengthens validation.

## Keep decisions accountable

AI can propose an interpretation. Named owners still need to decide whether a historical rule remains necessary, a control is adequate, or a trade-off is acceptable.

Across a legacy estate, preserve the resulting vocabulary, decision records, and test patterns for future work. Each modernization can improve understanding and verification across the estate.
