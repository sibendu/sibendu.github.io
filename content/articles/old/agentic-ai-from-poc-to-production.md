Enterprise AI succeeds when it is treated as architecture, not only experimentation.

My recent work has focused on taking Gen AI and agentic systems from concept to execution: building secure production patterns, integrating with legacy applications and business processes, adding observability, and measuring value in business terms.

## What matters in production

- Clear business workflow ownership
- A fit-for-purpose RAG and agentic architecture
- Evaluation design before broad rollout
- Guardrails for safety, governance, and compliance
- Observability across model, tool, workflow, and user behavior
- Adoption support for business and technology teams

## Practical framing

A credible AI transformation program needs more than a model selection exercise. It needs architecture decisions that connect business value, data readiness, integration constraints, security controls, and delivery assurance.
